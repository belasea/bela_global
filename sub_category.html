Urelia

Scaly skin. Hyperkeratosis

The key ingredient is urea! Urea is naturally present in the skin. It is unique, because depending on its concentration, it can either hydrate or exfoliate to reduce the thickness of scales.

In the Urelia range, it is associated with an amino acid complex to form a protective film on the skin's surface to limit dehydration and protect your skin from external aggressions.

The range includes two exfoliating moisturizers that treat hyperkeratosis no matter the degree of severity, as well as a cleansing gel for gently exfoliating and cleansing your body and scalp.
